How It Works

No commitment until your meet-and-greet

By filling out this form, you are reserving a spot in the clinic. You will not be charged until we have had an initial appointment and you have verified that you'd like to proceed.

After submitting, we'll review your information and schedule a meet-and-greet to verify the doctor-patient relationship is a good fit. If both parties agree, membership is confirmed.
