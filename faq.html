Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Direct Primary Care and BlueFire Med.

How is BlueFire Direct Care different than traditional medical clinics?+
BlueFire Direct Care uses Direct Primary Care (DPC) — doctors are paid directly by patients, not through insurance companies or government payers. We cut out the middleman. The DPC model is reminiscent of when doctors had time to spend with you, didn't have thousands of patients, and you didn't wait weeks for an appointment. Many of our patients have insurance but still save money and get better access by adding a BlueFire membership.
What can I expect as a patient?+
Shorter wait times, longer appointments, and increased access to your doctor. No more rushed visits or weeks-long waits. You can contact your physician via phone, social media, email, and text messaging day or night.
Do I still need insurance?+
We encourage patients to carry at minimum a major medical or catastrophic plan. Under ACA, having major medical coverage and a physician on retainer (like BlueFire Med) exempts you from the underinsured tax. A low-premium, high-deductible plan pairs perfectly with DPC. Why pay $200 toward a deductible when you can get the same test for $15?
Can my fees be reimbursed by my insurance company?+
In some cases, yes. You can submit receipts for membership fees or lab/medication costs. Some insurers apply these toward your deductible. Check your specific insurer's policies.
Can I use my Health Savings Account (HSA)?+
HSA funds can be used for medications, imaging, and labs at BlueFire Med. However, HSA funds currently cannot be used for DPC membership fees directly — though legislation is pending to change this.
How much will BlueFire Med cost?+
Monthly fees are based on age. We also have an on-site wholesale pharmacy — savings on prescriptions may cover the cost of your entire membership fee. Patients are offered wholesale cash prices on lab tests and imaging as well. See our Member Benefits page for full details.
How can you guarantee same or next-day appointments?+
BlueFire Med accepts only 600 patients — compared to the 2,000–4,000 that traditional doctors manage. Fewer patients means more scheduling flexibility. We keep our numbers low specifically so we can be there when you need us.
Will the doctors still help if I'm admitted to the hospital?+
Yes. The hospitalist manages day-to-day inpatient care, but our doctors are happy to visit and coordinate to make sure you understand what's happening and your care is properly managed.
Can I get all of my medications from BlueFire Med's pharmacy?+
As a member you pay only our cost — always below retail pharmacy prices. Nearly all medications are generic. We do not dispense DEA-controlled medications (narcotics, sedatives, stimulants). A small supply fee applies.
How can I contact my doctor after hours?+
For truly urgent needs outside office hours, call 785-539-9218 and leave a message — the on-call doctor will return your call. For routine questions, refills, or scheduling, email nurse@bluefiremed.com or call/text during business hours. (Texting only available during business hours.)
Are there medications the doctors do not prescribe?+
BlueFire is not accepting new patients for pharmaceutical treatment of Chronic Pain. Patients using narcotics for chronic pain are encouraged to seek a pain treatment center.
How does BlueFire Med handle vaccines?+
We recommend the local health department for most routine vaccines — they offer subsidized pricing depending on insurance status. All pediatric patients are required to follow current CDC vaccine guidelines. Exceptions are only made for documented medical reasons such as known anaphylaxis to a specific vaccine.
Are there any other fees I should expect?+
Almost all routine procedures are included in membership fees. There are a few exceptions for complicated procedures. Our membership information page has full pricing details.
Will BlueFire Med see non-members?+
We primarily treat members only, but can occasionally make exceptions. Call us to discuss your situation before coming in. Medication, lab, and imaging discounts are available to members only.
Do BlueFire Med doctors make house calls?+
Yes! The initial newborn visit is at no charge. All other house calls are $50. Visits outside office hours also carry a $50 charge at the time of the visit.
Are the physicians board certified?+
Yes. All physicians are board certified and licensed in the state of Kansas. Click here to learn more about our providers.
