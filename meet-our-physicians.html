Meet Our Physicians

Board-certified providers committed to practicing medicine the way it should be — with time, care, and genuine attention.

Andrew Pope, MD
Family Medicine · Founder

Dr. Pope has been a part of the Manhattan community since 2012. He attended the University of Kansas graduating with honors with dual-undergraduate degrees, and the KU School of Medicine. He completed the Via Christi Family Medicine residency with extensive training in outpatient, inpatient, ob/gyn, and procedures including colonoscopies and EGDs.

Board-certified in Family Medicine through the American Board of Family Medicine, he has also served as president of the Riley County Medical Society. In 2017, Dr. Pope realized his dream of opening a Direct Primary Care practice — providing modern evidence-based medicine delivered in an old-fashioned way.

Dr. Pope also offers PRP services at BlueFire Med for orthopedic issues, hair loss, and skin (PRP facials). Learn more at www.bluefireprp.com. He also founded BlueFire Supplements for joint pain, arthritis, and musculoskeletal support — www.bluefiresupplements.com.

Dr. Pope's practice is full, but contact the office at 785-539-9218 or nurse@bluefiremed.com — he is typically happy to accommodate requests.

Summer Huncovsky, APRN
Board-Certified Family Nurse Practitioner

Summer Huncovsky has lived in the Manhattan area for the past 17 years. She is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner by the American Academy of Nurse Practitioners with over 25 years in the healthcare industry. She holds an MSN from Washburn University and a BSN from Fort Hays State University.

She has 8 years of FNP experience in primary care, following years as a Registered Nurse in Family Medicine, Cardiology, Neurology, and Pediatrics, plus time in Urgent Care and Public Health. She loves Women's Health and caring for women in all phases of life.

Summer sees her role as a healthcare advocate — with the patient and family as the experts on themselves. She is married with two children and enjoys the outdoors and the family cattle operation. She is ecstatic to be joining the BlueFire Med team and changing healthcare for the better!

Mandi Diederich, PA-C
NCCPA Board-Certified Physician Assistant

Mandi is a NCCPA board-certified Physician Assistant and proud KSU alumna — a member of Kansas State University's inaugural Physician Assistant cohort. She graduated with her Master of Science in Physician Assistant Studies in 2024 and holds a BS from KSU and an AA from Washburn University.

A northeast Kansas native from rural Washington County, Mandi has 8 years of diverse healthcare experience. She has special interests in pediatric health, women's health, and orthopedic care, and loves building long-term relationships with patients across all stages of life.

Mandi is a wife, mother of two, devoted K-State fan, and outdoor enthusiast. She is excited to join the BlueFire Med team and provide optimal patient-centered care!
