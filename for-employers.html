For Employers

Save Money on Employee Healthcare

If you provide comprehensive insurance, you have the potential of saving 50% or more on healthcare benefits for your team.

How It Works

Better Care. Lower Cost.

If you can't afford ever-increasing premiums, we offer a way to provide exceptional care at a very affordable rate. We can offer "Wrap Around" coverage — a government-approved alternative that saves an average of 40–60% on health expenses, and comes with a discount on your BlueFire Med membership.

We work closely with an insurance broker who can typically find a plan with better coverage than your existing policy while also reducing your premiums. Contact us to learn more.

Zion HealthShare is a wonderful option for employer-sponsored healthcare with tailored plans that fit most any employer — especially worth exploring for companies with fewer than 50 employees.

Sedera is an innovative medical cost-sharing solution for employers. Paired with a DPC membership, they offer further discounts. Learn more at www.sedera.com/employers or email sammi.bessert@mintbenefits.com.

Health Reimbursement Accounts work great with DPC — allowing employers to use pre-tax dollars to fund the membership and other medical expenses. Contact our office to learn more.
